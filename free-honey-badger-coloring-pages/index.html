Free Honey Badger Coloring Pages for Kids

If your kid likes funny animal coloring pages, meet the honey badger with swagger. These three free printable pages come from Boss Badger, a coloring book for ages 6 to 12 where a tough, confident honey badger wears sunglasses, scratches records as a DJ and lands a kickflip at the skate park.

Real honey badgers, also called ratels, live in parts of Africa and Asia and have a reputation for being fearless and tough. They are also known for raiding beehives for honey and grubs. The book takes that attitude and turns it into goofy city scenes that older kids and tweens enjoy, with bold outlines and plenty of detail to shade. It makes a good rainy-day activity, a screen-free break or a birthday party printable. Print on US Letter paper at 100% scale and try markers, colored pencils or gel pens. Printing tips

  • Print at 100% scale on US Letter (8.5 x 11 in) paper. For A4, choose “Fit to page”.
  • Use heavier paper (65 lb / 120 gsm or more) if you color with markers, so ink does not bleed through.
  • Download the PDF first, then print from your PDF viewer for the sharpest lines.
