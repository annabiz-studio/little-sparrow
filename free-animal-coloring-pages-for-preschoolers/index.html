Free Animal Coloring Pages for Preschoolers

These free animal coloring pages for preschoolers are taken from Brave, Busy & Wise, a coloring book that pairs every animal with a word that describes it: the brave lion, the wise owl, the strong elephant. Each page has one big, friendly animal with thick outlines and large areas, so small hands can color without frustration.

Ask your child what the word means while they color. Why is the owl wise? When have you felt brave? Simple questions like these build vocabulary and talk about feelings, and they turn a quiet coloring session into a mini lesson for ages 3 to 6. The pages work well at home, in a preschool or kindergarten classroom, or for a calm-down corner. Print on US Letter paper at 100% scale and offer chunky crayons or washable markers. The complete book has 40 animals and traits to color and talk about.

Printing tips

  • Print at 100% scale on US Letter (8.5 x 11 in) paper. For A4, choose “Fit to page”.
  • Use heavier paper (65 lb / 120 gsm or more) if you color with markers, so ink does not bleed through.
  • Download the PDF first, then print from your PDF viewer for the sharpest lines.
