Free Mandala Animal Coloring Pages

These free mandala animal coloring pages are for teens and adults who like detailed, patterned art. Each page comes from Mandala Animals, a mindful coloring book in which an animal is built from layers of circles, petals and flowing shapes. Your free set has an owl, a sea turtle and a peacock.

The word mandala comes from Sanskrit and means circle, and many people enjoy coloring them because the repeating patterns invite slow, focused attention. Start from the center and work outward, or pick a small palette of three or four colors and repeat them for a harmonious result. Fine-tip markers, gel pens and sharp colored pencils all work well. Print on US Letter paper at 100% scale, and use thicker paper if you want to blend or use markers. The complete book includes 40 animal mandalas, from foxes and whales to hummingbirds and koi, so there is always another design waiting.

Printing tips

  • Print at 100% scale on US Letter (8.5 x 11 in) paper. For A4, choose “Fit to page”.
  • Use heavier paper (65 lb / 120 gsm or more) if you color with markers, so ink does not bleed through.
  • Download the PDF first, then print from your PDF viewer for the sharpest lines.
